High-Value Risk Premium & Terms

  • $200 – $1,000 raw: Base labor rate + 1.5%–2% of declared raw value.
  • $1,000+ raw: $50–$75/hr labor + 2%–3% risk insurance fee.
  • Evaluation fee ($5–$10): Applied only if a card is non-correctable — credited toward the work if you proceed.
  • Bulk savings (10+ cards): 30%–40% discount on batch curve flattening or cleaning.
  • Grading note: Non-invasive cleaning & curve correction routinely pass PSA/CGC/BGS. Heavy crease tooling carries a minor "Altered" risk if over-processed.
